The Space

We designed the room before we wrote the menu.

Raghunathpur had plenty of places to eat and nowhere to stay. So we built the opposite — low light, long tables, no television, and a terrace that opens at six-thirty when the heat finally lets go.

  • An open, coal-fired pass

    The tandoor and the grill sit in full view. Nothing leaves the kitchen you cannot watch being made.

  • Sourced inside Odisha

    Produce from Khordha and Cuttack markets, fish from Chilika, paneer set in-house every morning.

  • Eighteen dishes, not eighty

    A short menu we can actually cook well, rewritten four times a year.
