    Call or send the list

    Tell us the dates, the location and roughly what you are shooting. A tick-list from the rental catalogue is enough.

  2. 02

Questions

Camera rental in Bhubaneswar: common questions

The eight things people ask before they book. If yours is not here, call +91 82600 76264.

How much does camera rental cost in Bhubaneswar?

Rates depend on the body, the glass and how many days you need. Send us the kit list and dates and you get an itemised quote the same day, covering equipment, crew and transport. Weekly and monthly holds cost less per day than single-day hire.

Do you deliver camera equipment outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. We dispatch across Odisha — Cuttack, Puri, Konark, Chilika, Berhampur, Sambalpur and Rourkela are all regular runs. Transport is quoted as a separate line so you can see exactly what it costs.

Can I rent a camera with an operator or a full crew?

Yes. Hire equipment on its own, equipment with an operator, or a full unit — director of photography, focus puller, gaffer and sparks, drone pilot, sound recordist, DIT and production assistants.

Which cinema cameras do you have on rent?

Sony BURANO, four FX6, seven FX3, FX2, A7S III, A7 IV and A7 V bodies, plus Canon EOS R3, R5 C, R6 Mark II and C50 — twenty-five bodies in total.

Do you rent cine lenses in Odisha?

Yes. The Zeiss Nano Prime set at T1.5 (18, 24, 35, 50, 75 and 100mm) and the Zeiss CP.3 PL-mount set (15mm T2.9 and 25, 35, 50, 85mm at T2.1). Proper PL cine glass is rare in Odisha — most shoots here are still cut with stills lenses.

Do you provide drone shoots in Odisha?

Yes. DJI Mavic 3 Cine with Apple ProRes, Mavic 3 Pro and Mavic 3 Classic, flown by our own pilot. Tell us the location when you book so any permissions can be sorted before the shoot day.

What is the minimum rental period?

One day is the standard unit, and a day means a shoot day rather than a fixed number of hours. For long productions we hold kit by the week or the month.

What do you need from me to confirm a booking?

Photo ID, the shoot dates and location, and a confirmed kit list. Deposit and payment terms are set out on the quote before anything is dispatched.
